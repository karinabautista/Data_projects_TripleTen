## Product-Level Profit
A clear breakpoint appears around -$1,000 in profit, where losses become significantly steeper.

Approximately 20 products fall below this threshold, representing the most consistent and impactful losses.

**Insight:**
A small group of products is responsible for a disproportionate share of total losses.

**Recommendation:**
Prioritize reviewing or discontinuing these high-loss products to reduce ongoing financial impact. This allows the business to stabilize profitability while maintaining the majority of profitable or minimally unprofitable items.

## Advertising  
The strongest state–month combinations for advertising are:
- Indiana (October)
- Vermont (November)
- Washington (March)

Using a return on ad spend (ROAS) target of 1:5, the recommended maximum ad spend is:
- $128.63 (Indiana – October)
- $119.19 (Vermont – November)
- $104.27 (Washington – March)

**Insight:**
Certain time periods and regions generate significantly higher returns, making them ideal for targeted advertising.

**Recommendation:**
Focus advertising efforts on high-performing state–month combinations to maximize return on investment rather than spreading budget evenly.

## Conclusions & Recommendations

This analysis shows that profitability is not evenly distributed across products, categories, or regions. A small number of products and sub-categories drive the majority of both profits and losses.

High-performing areas such as Technology, Copiers, and Phones provide strong returns and should remain a focus. At the same time, consistent losses in products like Tables, Binders, and certain Office Supplies highlight clear opportunities for improvement.

Returns further impact profitability, with certain products and customers contributing disproportionately to return rates. Additionally, targeted advertising opportunities demonstrate that strategic investment can significantly improve returns.

### Key Recommendations
**Remove or improve high-loss products:**
Focus on the 20 products generating the largest losses to quickly reduce negative financial impact.

**Reevaluate underperforming sub-categories:**
Consider restructuring, repositioning, or discontinuing categories that consistently operate at a loss.

**Prioritize high-performing categories:**
Continue investing in Technology and other strong performers to maximize profitability.

**Optimize advertising spend:**
Allocate budget to high-performing state–month combinations to improve return on ad spend.

**Investigate high return rate products:**
Address potential quality, listing, or fulfillment issues—especially for products with 100% return rates.

**Analyze customer return behavior:**
Explore whether high-return customers indicate broader issues or opportunities to refine return policies.
